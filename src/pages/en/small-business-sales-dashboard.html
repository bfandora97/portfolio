Small Business Sales Dashboard

Problem

The owner recapped sales every week. The data lived in 4 places, each with its own format.

Scattered sources

  • POS exports from 2 outlets, each in its own Excel file.
  • GoFood/GrabFood reports downloaded separately from the partner apps.
  • Shopee order reports with different columns and product names again.

The cost

  • About 4 hours every week of copy-paste, matching product names and recalculating.
  • Inconsistent product names ("Es Kopsu", "ES KOPI SUSU SENJA", "Kopi Susu Es"), so per-product totals rarely matched.
  • App commissions were never deducted, so margin per channel was invisible.

What I did

Merge and clean

  • Combined 4 sources (2 outlets' POS, GoFood/GrabFood, Shopee) into one sales table.
  • Standardized product names with a mapping table, so each product has one name across every channel.
  • Added cost of goods per product and commission per channel to calculate gross margin.

Automatic updates

  • All data lands in Google Sheets as the single source of truth.
  • The Looker Studio dashboard reads that sheet and refreshes daily.
  • The owner opens one link. No more manual recap in Excel.

Example insights

  • Online channels earn thinner margins because of app commissions: about 41% on GoFood/GrabFood versus 61% in the outlets.
  • 2 slow sellers (Red Velvet Latte and Kue Lumpur Pandan) are flagged to drop so stock stops piling up.
  • Peak hours are 3–6 pm every day, busiest on weekends: the right time for extra staff or promotions.

What the dashboard shows

  • Total sales, number of transactions, average ticket and gross margin.
  • Monthly revenue by channel and the top 5 products.
  • A busy-hours heatmap by weekday and a table of slow-moving stock.

Kopi Senja is a fictional business. All figures were created for this demo.
