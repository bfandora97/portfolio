FAQ

How do we work together?

Through Upwork. Fixed-price milestones, and your payment is held by Upwork until you approve the work. We talk in Upwork chat; calls can be arranged.

What is your time zone?

Jakarta, GMT+7. I reply within 24 hours on weekdays, usually much sooner. My working hours overlap with European afternoons and US East Coast mornings.

Is my data safe?

Yes. Your data is used only for your project, never shared, and deleted after delivery if you ask. I am happy to sign an NDA.

Do I need to buy software?

Not necessarily. Google Sheets and Looker Studio are free. Power BI is free to build in, but sharing reports privately usually needs a Power BI Pro licence.

What if I need changes later?

Every package includes 2 rounds of revisions. For ongoing updates, the monthly reporting plan keeps your dashboard current.

Need a dashboard or cleaner data?

Send a sample of your data and what you want to track. I will tell you what I can build and how long it takes.
